## 1. Purpose

Define the Results Screen shown after every race: who is shown, what information is displayed, player actions, result integrity rules, and a **placeholder only** for future rewards.

## 10. Acceptance Criteria

P011 v1.0 is satisfied when all of the following are true:

1. Results Screen appears immediately after every race; all four players shown; ordered by final race position.  
2. Displayed fields are only: Final Position, Player Name, Selected Character, Race Time.  
3. Rewards exist as a placeholder for a future system; no reward currencies or progression invented.  
4. Player actions are only: Continue, Return to Main Menu.  
5. Results cannot be modified by players; official result is server-generated.  
6. Listed ?Not Defined? topics are not invented.  
7. Future dependencies, open questions, and acceptance criteria are present.  
8. Document version is **P011 v1.0**.
